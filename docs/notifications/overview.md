# Emails & Notifications Overview

MailPrism reaches you in four ways: the **bell** in the app, short **toasts**
after you act, **emails** from MailPrism, and messages it posts to your **chat
apps**. This page lists all of them in one place, with the setting that controls
each one.

---

## Emails from MailPrism

These come from **MailPrism &lt;noreply@mail.mailprism.ai&gt;**.

| Email | You get it when | Turn it off in |
|-------|-----------------|----------------|
| [Notification alert](./alerts-and-digests.md#notification-alerts) | Something happens in a category you set to **Email** | Settings → Notifications → Category Preferences |
| [Daily digest](./alerts-and-digests.md#daily-digest) | Once a day, at the hour you pick | Settings → Notifications → Email Digest |
| [Weekly activity report](./alerts-and-digests.md#weekly-activity-report) | Once a week (currently paused) | Settings → Notifications |
| [Rule notification](./rule-notification-emails.md) | A rule with the **Send Me a Notification Email** action matches | Edit the rule and remove the action |
| [Workspace invitation](./account-emails.md#workspace-invitation) | Someone invites you to their workspace | Not optional |
| [Forwarding address verification](./account-emails.md#forwarding-address-verification) | You add a forwarding address | Not optional |
| [Payment failed](./account-emails.md#payment-failed) | Your card is declined | Not optional |
| [Beta invite](./account-emails.md#beta-invite) | You're let in from the beta waitlist | Not optional |

## Emails about your sign-in

Confirm your email, reset your password, confirm an email change, and one-time
sign-in links. See [Sign-in emails](./account-emails.md#sign-in-emails).

## Emails sent from your own Gmail

Some features send mail **as you**, from your connected Gmail account. They
appear in your Gmail **Sent** folder. See
[Emails sent from your Gmail](./sent-from-your-gmail.md).

| Feature | What it sends |
|---------|---------------|
| [Auto-responder](../tracking/auto-responders.md) | Your vacation or after-hours reply |
| [Nudges](../tracking/nudges-and-reminders.md) | Follow-ups on emails nobody answered |
| [Rules](../rules/actions.md) | Forwards, auto-replies and scheduled sends you set up |
| [Bulk Unsubscribe](../inbox/unsubscribe.md) | Unsubscribe requests to mailing lists |

## In the app

| Where | What |
|-------|------|
| [The bell](./in-app.md#the-bell) | Recent notifications, with an unread count |
| [Notifications page](./in-app.md#the-notifications-page) | Every notification, filterable by category |
| [Toasts](./in-app.md#toasts) | Short confirmations and errors after you act, often with **Undo** |
| [Banners](./in-app.md#banners) | Account-wide problems, such as a Gmail account that needs reconnecting |

## Chat apps and webhooks

Rules can post a matching email to Slack, Discord, Microsoft Teams, Google Chat
or your own webhook. See [Chat & webhook messages](./chat-and-webhooks.md).
