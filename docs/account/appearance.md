# Appearance

Make MailPrism comfortable to look at. Everything here lives under **Settings →
Appearance**, with a **Live Preview** at the top so you can see each change before
you save.

:::info Saved to this browser
Appearance preferences are stored **locally in your browser**, not your account.
They take full effect once you click **Save Changes**. Set them again on another
device or browser if you want the same look there.
:::

---

## Theme

Choose your color scheme:

- **Light** — best for daytime.
- **Dark** — easier on the eyes.
- **System** — matches your device's setting and switches automatically.

---

## Density

Adjust how tightly content is packed:

| Option | Feel |
|--------|------|
| **Compact** | More content, less spacing |
| **Comfortable** | Balanced spacing (recommended) |
| **Spacious** | More breathing room |

---

## Font size

Pick a comfortable reading size: **Small**, **Medium** (recommended), or **Large**.
The Live Preview updates to show the new size.

---

## Labels

- **Show Gmail system labels** — display Gmail's built-in labels (INBOX, IMPORTANT,
  and so on) on your emails. This is **off by default**, because most people find
  them noisy. Turn it on if you rely on Gmail's own labelling.

---

## Accessibility

Two options to make the interface easier to use:

- **Reduce motion** — minimizes animations and transitions throughout MailPrism.
- **High contrast** — increases color contrast for better visibility.

:::tip Reduce motion follows your needs
If animations are distracting or uncomfortable, turn **Reduce motion** on for a
calmer, steadier interface.
:::

---

## Live preview & saving

The **Live Preview** card at the top of the page shows a sample email styled with
your current theme, density, and font size — so you can confirm the look before
committing.

- **Save Changes** applies your selections.
- **Reset** discards unsaved edits and returns to your last saved settings.
