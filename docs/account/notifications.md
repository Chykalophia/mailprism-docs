# Notifications

Decide how and when MailPrism updates you — in the app, by email digest, or both.
Everything lives under **Settings → Notifications**. Use **Save Changes** to
apply your edits.

---

## In-app notifications

The **In-App Notifications** toggle turns the in-product notification feed on or
off. When it's on, MailPrism shows alerts inside the app for activity and updates
as they happen.

---

## Email digest

An email digest is a periodic **summary** email — separate from immediate alerts.
Choose a frequency:

| Option | What you get |
|--------|--------------|
| **No digest emails** | No summary emails. (Immediate per-category alerts are still available — see below.) |
| **Daily digest** | A summary every day at your chosen time. |
| **Weekly digest** | A summary every Monday at your chosen time. *(Recommended.)* |

### Delivery time & timezone

When the digest is set to Daily or Weekly, a **Delivery time** dropdown appears.
Pick the hour you'd like it to arrive. The time is interpreted in **your
timezone**, which is shown beneath the dropdown.

---

## Weekly activity report

The **Weekly activity report** toggle sends a summary of your automation each
week — rules executed, emails processed, and AI usage. It's a quick way to see
what MailPrism did on your behalf.

---

## Category preferences

Open **Category Preferences → Show advanced settings** to fine-tune notifications
per category. For each category you can independently toggle:

- <span class="mp-pill mp-pill--blue">In-app</span> — whether it appears in the
  in-product feed.
- <span class="mp-pill mp-pill--violet">Email</span> — whether it triggers an
  immediate email alert.

The categories are:

| Category | Covers |
|----------|--------|
| **Rule Execution** | When your rules run on emails. |
| **Response Tracking** | Changes to conversation follow-up states. |
| **AI Insights** | AI suggestions and observations. |
| **System** | General system messages. |
| **System Alerts** | Important system-level alerts. |
| **Billing** | Plan, payment, and invoice events. |
| **Security** | Sign-in and account security events. |
| **New Features** | Newly shipped features. |
| **Product Updates** | Product news and changes. |
| **Digest** | Digest-related messages. |
| **Label Conflicts** | When synced labels clash across accounts. |

:::tip Turn off email, keep in-app
Set a category's **Email** toggle off but leave **In-app** on to keep seeing it
in the app without filling your inbox.
:::

---

## Good to know

- Notifications respect your device's **Do Not Disturb** settings.
- Email digests and reports are sent to your **primary email address**.
- A digest of **No digest emails** still allows immediate per-category email
  alerts — the two settings are independent.
