# Your Dashboard

The dashboard is your home base after signing in. It answers one question at a
glance: **what's happening with my email right now?** This page tours the dashboard
widgets, then points you to the main areas of the app.

## At a glance

The top of the dashboard shows four stat cards:

| Stat | What it means |
|------|----------------|
| **Needs Action** | Emails that look like they need a reply or action from you. |
| **Awaiting Reply** | Emails where you're waiting on someone else to respond. |
| **Rules Triggered** | How many times your automations ran today. |
| **Active Rules** | How many of your rules are currently switched on. |

The first two cards show a small **sparkline** — a recent trend line — so you can
see whether a number is climbing or settling. Each card links straight to the
matching view, so you can go from a number to the actual emails behind it in one
click.

*Needs Action* and *Awaiting Reply* come from response tracking — see
**[Response tracking](../tracking/overview.md)**.

## AI insights

An **AI Insights** widget surfaces recent AI activity in your inbox. It's still
rolling out — its toggle in Dashboard Preferences is currently marked
**Coming Soon** — so it may not be available in your account yet. See
**[AI Insights](../ai/insights.md)**.

## Recent activity

A running list of your most recent rule executions — which rule fired, on which
email, and when. Use **View all** to open the full **Rule Logs**, where you can
inspect or undo what a rule did.

## Quick actions

Shortcut buttons for the things you do most:

- **View Inbox** — open your mail.
- **Check Replies** — jump to *Needs Action* and *Awaiting Reply*.
- **New Rule** — start building an automation.

## Getting around the app

The dashboard is one stop. Here's the rest of the map:

| Area | What it's for |
|------|----------------|
| **Inbox** | Your mail — read, reply, label, archive, and act on messages. See **[Inbox overview](../inbox/overview.md)**. |
| **Replies** | Conversations grouped by what they need from you — *Needs Action* and *Awaiting Reply*. See **[Response tracking](../tracking/overview.md)**. |
| **Rules** | Build, test, and manage your automations. See **[How rules work](../rules/overview.md)**. |
| **Analytics** | What your automations have done, over time. See **[Analytics](../analytics.md)**. |
| **Settings** | Connect accounts, tune AI, set scheduling, and more. See **[Connecting Gmail](./connecting-gmail.md)** and **[Preferences](../account/preferences.md)**. |

### Command palette

Anywhere in the app, press **Cmd + K** (Mac) or **Ctrl + K** (Windows / Linux) to open
the **command palette**. Type what you're after — *archive*, *go to rules*, *API keys*
— and pick it from the list, without reaching for the mouse.

To search your mail instead, press **/** on the email pages to jump to the inbox
search box.

→ Full shortcut list: **[Keyboard shortcuts](../inbox/keyboard-shortcuts.md)**

## Make it yours

The dashboard is customizable. You can:

- **Show or hide** each widget (stats, AI insights, recent activity, quick actions).
- Choose a **layout density** — compact or comfortable.
- Set an **auto-refresh** interval, which only refreshes while the tab is focused
  to save resources.

You'll find these under **[Preferences](../account/preferences.md)**.

:::info First time here?
If Gmail isn't connected yet, the dashboard shows a prompt to connect it. Start

:::
