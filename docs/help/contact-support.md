# Contact Support

Stuck? A person on the MailPrism team reads every message. Before you write,
[Troubleshooting](./troubleshooting.md) and the [FAQ](./faq.md) answer the most
common questions.

<Intro>
<InShort>

Open **Help** in the app's sidebar, scroll to **Still need help?**, click
**Contact Support**, and send a subject and message. We reply to your account
email address.

</InShort>
<BeforeYouStart>

- What you were trying to do, and what happened instead.
- Any error message, word for word, or a screenshot.

</BeforeYouStart>
</Intro>

## Open Help

Click **Help** in the app's sidebar. The **Help Center** opens, with a search
box and guides by category.

</Step>
<Step hint="Contact Support opens a short form.">

## Open the contact form

Scroll to **Still need help?** and click **Contact Support**.

</Step>
<Step hint="A clear subject gets you a faster answer.">

## Write your message

Add a **Subject**, such as *Rule not archiving newsletters*, and describe the
problem in **Message**: what you did, what you expected, and what happened.
Never include passwords or recovery codes.

</Step>
<Step hint="We reply to your account email address.">

## Send it

Click **Send Message**. You'll see **Message Sent!** There's no automatic
receipt. A person replies to your account email, usually from
`hello@mailprism.ai`.

</Step>
</Steps>

<DoneWhen>

You see **Message Sent!** in the app.

</DoneWhen>

## If the form doesn't work

If the form says it couldn't send your message, or that you've sent several
messages recently, email [hello@mailprism.ai](mailto:hello@mailprism.ai)
directly. Can't sign in at all? Email the same address from the address you use
for MailPrism.
