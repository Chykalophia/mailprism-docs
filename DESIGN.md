# MailPrism Docs: Design System and Style Guide

Redesigned November 2026. The docs read like a calm SaaS help centre: a white
page, one indigo accent, serif page titles, and content placed in cards only
where a card means something. The prism spectrum appears only as the 2px line
under the navbar and as step numerals.

Files:

| File | What lives there |
|------|------------------|
| `src/css/tokens.css` | Every color, font, radius, shadow and motion value, for light, dark and high contrast |
| `src/css/custom.css` | Infima mapping, navbar, sidebar, article type, admonitions, tables, footer, `.mp-pill` |
| `src/css/reading.css` | What each Reading option changes |
| `src/css/sidebar-icons.css` | Generated by `node scripts/build-sidebar-icons.mjs` from `src/components/Icon/icons.json` |

---

## 1. Principles

1. **Answer first.** Every guide opens with *In short*: the whole task in two or
   three sentences.
2. **One accent.** Indigo marks what you can click. Meaning colors (tip, done,
   warning, danger) appear only on their admonitions and states.
3. **Cards mean something.** In short, Before you start, steps in Step by step
   view, You're done when, At a glance. Plain prose is never boxed.
4. **The reader chooses how to read.** Read, Step by step or Walkthrough per
   guide, and Reading options for size, spacing, font, contrast and motion.
5. **Every step leads to the app.** Steps and settings carry *Open in MailPrism*
   deep links.

## 2. Tokens

Change values only in `tokens.css`. All text pairs meet WCAG 4.5:1 and all
control boundaries (`--mp-border-strong`) meet 3:1 in light, dark and both high
contrast themes. Recheck with `python3 scripts/check-contrast.py` after any change.

| Token | Light | Dark | Use |
|-------|-------|------|-----|
| `--mp-bg` | `#ffffff` | `#0b0d12` | Page |
| `--mp-surface` | `#f7f8fa` | `#11141b` | Quiet panels, footer |
| `--mp-card` | `#ffffff` | `#151922` | Cards |
| `--mp-border` | `#e4e6ec` | `#232835` | Hairlines (decorative) |
| `--mp-border-strong` | `#858c9e` | `#6b7386` | Inputs, buttons, switches (3:1) |
| `--mp-text` / `-2` / `-3` | `#0e1120` / `#4a5163` / `#5f6779` | `#eceef4` / `#b4bac8` / `#9aa1b2` | Body, secondary, meta |
| `--mp-accent` | `#4f46e5` | `#a5b4fc` | Links, primary buttons, current state |
| `--mp-tip`, `--mp-done`, `--mp-warn`, `--mp-danger` | teal, green, amber, red | lighter tints | Admonitions and states only |
| `--mp-n1`…`--mp-n4` | violet, blue, cyan, green | lighter tints | Step numerals, cycled |

## 3. Typography

- **Page titles (H1, 38px and up):** Instrument Serif, weight 400. Never smaller.
- **Everything else:** Inter Variable. H2 1.45rem with a hairline above, H3 1.15rem.
- **Labels** (card labels, At a glance, legends): JetBrains Mono, 0.72rem, uppercase.
- Body 17px, line-height 1.7, measure 72ch. Reading options scale all three.
- Reading option fonts: Lexend and Atkinson Hyperlegible Next. When chosen, the
  serif titles switch to that font too.

## 4. Writing a guide

A task guide has this shape. All components are global in MDX; no imports.

```mdx
---
title: Forward Email to Another Address
glance:
  time: 5 minutes
  plan: Every plan            # optional
  where: [Settings, Forwarding Rules]
  app: /settings/forwarding   # app path for Open in MailPrism
  appLabel: Open Forwarding Rules
---

# Forward Email to Another Address

One-paragraph intro.

<Intro>
<InShort>

The whole task in two or three sentences.

</InShort>
<BeforeYouStart>

- What the reader needs first.

</BeforeYouStart>
</Intro>

<Steps>
<Step
  app="/settings/forwarding"
  shot="/img/screens/integrations/forwarding.webp"
  shotAlt="What the screenshot shows"
  spot={{x: 85.8, y: 5.8, w: 10.6, h: 5.4}}
  hint="Walkthrough bubble text.">

## Add the address

Step body in Markdown.

</Step>
</Steps>

<DoneWhen>

How the reader knows it worked.

</DoneWhen>
```

Rules:

- Each `<Step>` **must** start with a `## Heading`. It becomes the step title, its
  `#anchor`, and its entry in the table of contents.
- Leave a blank line after every opening tag and before every closing tag, or
  the Markdown inside won't render.
- `spot` is in percent of the screenshot: left, top, width, height. Measure on
  the 2400×1500 image.
- A step without `shot` keeps the previous step's screen in the Walkthrough. If
  no step has a shot, the Walkthrough view is hidden.
- `glance` fills the *At a glance* panel on the right (top of the page on phones).
- Deep links: `?view=steps` or `?view=walk` open a view; `#heading-id` opens that step.
- Only state a plan, time or limit you've checked in the app.

Other components:

- `<OpenInApp to="/settings/security">Open</OpenInApp>`: an inline deep link chip.
- `<Screenshot src=… srcDark=… alt=… caption=… />`: a framed screenshot.
- `<span class="mp-pill mp-pill--green">Positive</span>`: status pills
  (`violet`, `blue`, `green`, `amber`, `red`, `gray`).

## 5. Reading options

Saved in `localStorage` (`mp-reading`) and applied as `data-mp-*` attributes on
`<html>` by an inline script before first paint, so nothing jumps.

| Option | Attribute | Effect |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| Text size A−/A/A+/A++ | `data-mp-size` | Scales body, headings and cards |
| Spacing Normal/Roomy/Airy | `data-mp-spacing` | Line height, paragraph gap, narrower measure |
| Font Inter/Lexend/Hyperlegible | `data-mp-font` | Body and title font |
| Theme Light/Dark/High contrast | Docusaurus color mode + `data-mp-contrast` | High contrast darkens text and borders |
| Reading ruler | (component) | A band that follows the pointer or focus |
| Hide tips and extras | `data-mp-extras` | Hides tip, note and info admonitions and `.mp-extra` |
| Underline all links | `data-mp-links` | Underlines navigation links too |
| Reduce motion | `data-mp-motion` | Stops transitions, on top of the OS setting |

Step-by-step ticks are saved per page (`mp-done:<path>`), and the last view
chosen is remembered (`mp-guide-view`).

## 6. Accessibility and neurodivergent readability

These are requirements, not suggestions.

- **WCAG 2.2 AA.** 4.5:1 text, 3:1 controls, a visible 2px focus ring, targets of
  at least 24px (most are 32 to 40px), no information by color alone.
- **Chunk it.** Short paragraphs, one idea per section, the same page shape for
  every guide.
- **Predictable motion.** Small, and off under `prefers-reduced-motion` or the
  Reduce motion option.
- **Plain language.** Active voice, concrete examples, define jargon on first use.
- **Keyboard.** Every switcher, step button and Reading option works with Tab,
  Space/Enter and arrow keys; Escape closes Reading options and returns focus.

## 7. Writing voice (from MailPrism brand)

- Clear, not clever. Confident, not arrogant. Helpful, not pushy.
- **Do:** active voice · be specific · lead with the benefit · show examples.
- **Don't:** unexplained jargon · overpromise · fluff · competitor bashing.
- **Never publish unverified specifics.** Prices, quotas, and limits live on the
  live pricing/billing pages — link to them rather than hardcoding numbers that go
  stale or wrong.
